TOA MAKAPELU

The artist behind Angel Teeth.

Toa Makapelu is an artist and tattooist working under the name Angel Teeth. His creative journey began with painting, studying art for three years in Hawke's Bay before continuing his artistic development through further study and studio practice.

ARTISTIC APPROACH

MY WORK & STYLE

For Toa, painting and tattooing are different expressions of the same creative process. His work is driven by instinct, experimentation and a preference for bold, expressive imagery.

Influenced by the dramatic compositions and darker themes of artists such as Francisco Goya, Toa embraces scale, texture and movement. His background in oil painting continues to shape his artistic approach, while his tattooing process remains flexible, ranging from freehand designs drawn directly onto the skin to carefully prepared artwork developed with the client.
